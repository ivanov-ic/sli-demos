Solids, Liquids and Interfaces

Eight interactive demos for Lectures 5 to 8, on spatially variant systems and transport in solution. Each one runs entirely in the browser, needs no network connection once loaded, and can be driven live from a lecture theatre or explored alone.

Lecture 5 · Ions in solution

  • L5.1
    Coulomb against thermal energy

    Why salts dissolve in water as free ions and in hexane not at all.

  • L5.2
    Ionic atmosphere explorer

    Raise the ionic strength a hundredfold and the atmosphere shrinks only tenfold.

Lecture 6 · Non-ideality in electrolyte solutions

  • L6.1
    Activity coefficient models

    Which assumption fails first, and what the extra range costs.

  • L6.2
    Counting the free water

    How the activity coefficient gets above 1, and the lithium to potassium ordering.

Lecture 7 · Transport I: migration and conductivity

  • L7.1
    Extrapolating to the limiting value

    Why the law of independent migration is needed rather than merely available.

  • L7.2
    The ion race

    The hydrodynamic radius, the two roles of charge, and the Grotthuss anomaly.

Lecture 8 · Transport II: diffusion and thermal gradients

  • L8.1
    The diffusion clock

    A thousandfold increase in distance costs a millionfold increase in time.

  • L8.2
    Thermophoresis and an MST titration

    How a depletion measurement becomes a dissociation constant.
